The Pure / Corrupted system

Paladeux was developed around the theme “Loss of Control”. I was the only programmer on the project.

The player controls a cursed paladin with two states. In his Pure state, he retains control of his actions. When he becomes Corrupted, the character automatically attacks nearby enemies.

Both states also have their own progression, which led me to develop two experience and upgrade systems capable of working together.

A selection of the project's main scripts is publicly available.
