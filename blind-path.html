Presentation

Blind Path is a game in which you play as a man with a sad past, lost in the dark, blind to the path he must follow.

With a flashlight in his left hand, the player can see what lies ahead and progress through dark environments. In his right hand, a device warns him when the source of his fears approaches the sensors placed beforehand.

The project is based on exploration, solving situations and the need to hide in the darkness the player fears.

Free yourself from your fears and perhaps you will finally find a meaning to what darkens your mind.
