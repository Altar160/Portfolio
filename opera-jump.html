Rhythm-based gameplay

Opera Jump 20 was created solo in only 16 hours as part of a sprint based on the theme “Trouble / Disorder”.

The project combines narrative and platforming. Some gameplay sequences rely on events synchronized with the music, requiring the player to adapt their movements to the rhythm.

Part of the work therefore consisted of setting up and adjusting the different timings so that the gameplay elements remained synchronized with the musical progression.

I also developed the dialogue system as well as a complete localisation allowing the project to be played in French or English.
