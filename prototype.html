Gameplay

The cameras

Camera system, allows the player to switch cameras with left click and right click. If the camera has the monster in its field of view, the monster becomes immobilized but the camera becomes scrambled. If it becomes completely scrambled, it becomes out of order, but if the player switches cameras before that or puts away the phone, it "repairs" itself.

The drawings

The player has access to several drawings scattered around the map. If the player presses Space, they hold the drawing in their hand, immobilizing the monster NO MATTER WHERE IT IS. The only drawback is the duration: the drawing burns and the player does not have an unlimited quantity of them.

The tutorial

Tutorial, the monster can be seen on a TV, indirectly showing the camera system.

My contributions

As a second-session project, I kept the theme of the TFC, Survival Horror from an FPS perspective in Unity 6.5, showing only features that I find original and interesting to explore.

The player is trapped in a maze with a creature chasing them. They have access to surveillance cameras and consumable drawings that can temporarily immobilize the monster. Their apparent objective is to lure the creature into a containment area. However, the ending reveals that the player had actually been inside the creature's cage from the very beginning, as part of an experiment.
